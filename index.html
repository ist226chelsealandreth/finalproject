About Me

My name is Chelsea Landreth, and I love being active and spending time outdoors. Some of my favorite things to do are playing volleyball, going to the mountains and hunting the waterfalls. I also enjoy fishing and traveling to new places whenever I get the chance.

This portfolio represents the work I completed in IST 226. Throughout this course I learned how to build webpages, style them with CSS, organize layouts, and create more attractive and functional websites.

Being in this class really helped me learn how to create websites, and I actually started enjoying it more than I expected. One of my favorite things is thinking about making websites based on my travels, like creating pages for different places I have been, adding pictures, videos, and kind of turning it into something like a travel blog or memory page.
